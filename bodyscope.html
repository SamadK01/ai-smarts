Heart دل (قلب)

Cor
Circulatory System Thorax / Mediastinum Long Bone
Function & Physiology

Four-chambered muscular pump that circulates oxygenated blood through the systemic circulation and deoxygenated blood through the pulmonary circuit.

Clinical Facts

Beats approximately 100,000 times daily, pumping about 7,500 liters (2,000 gallons) of blood.

Common Conditions: Coronary artery disease, Arrhythmias, Heart failure, Valvular stenosis.
