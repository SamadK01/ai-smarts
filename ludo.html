Official Ludo Rules 📜
• Need a 6 to Release: Roll a 6 to move a token from base to the starting track.

• Bonus Extra Roll: Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent's token, or entering a token into Home grants an extra roll!

• Safe Star Spots (⭐): The 4 starting arrow cells and 4 midway star cells are safe — tokens cannot be cut there.

• Capture / Cut: Land on an opponent on an unstarred cell to send their token back to base!

• Three 6s Rule: Rolling three 6s consecutively forfeits the turn.

• Championship Victory: First player to guide all 4 tokens into the central Home Triangle wins the Championship! 👑
