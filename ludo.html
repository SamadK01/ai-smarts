Official Ludo Rules 📜
• 🎲 1 Die vs 🎲🎲 2 Dice Mode: Switch between Classic 1-Die and Fast-Paced 2-Dice ("Double Chakka") Mode at any time!

• 2-Dice Double Rules: In 2-Dice mode, roll both dice at once! Use values on different tokens or combine them. Any 6 opens a token from base. Rolling doubles (e.g. 5-5 or 6-6) grants a bonus extra roll!

• Fair-Entropy Dice Engine: Powered by cryptographic randomness with anti-repetition protection — no numbers get artificially stuck repeating over and over.

• Need a 6 to Release: Roll a 6 to move a token from base to the starting track.

• Bonus Extra Roll: Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent's token, or entering a token into Home grants an extra roll!

• Safe Star Spots (⭐): The 4 starting arrow cells and 4 midway star cells are safe — tokens cannot be cut there.

• Capture / Cut: Land on an opponent on an unstarred cell to send their token back to base!

• Three 6s Rule: In 1-Die mode, rolling three 6s consecutively forfeits the turn.

• Championship Victory: First player to guide all 4 tokens into the central Home Triangle wins the Championship! 👑
