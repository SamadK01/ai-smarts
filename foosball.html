How To Play

Master authentic table soccer physics with controllable rod slide, kick charging, and visible hands.

↕️
Slide Active Rod
Move Mouse Up/Down or press [W] / [S]. Rods follow with critically-damped smoothing and a natural speed limit.
⚡
Kick & Charge Power
Left Click or [Space] swings the rod forward in 0.15s and returns in 0.20s. Hold up to 0.5s to charge maximum power!
🔄
Change Active Rod
Scroll Mouse Wheel, press [1 - 4], or [A] / [D]. The visible hand glides smoothly across the table to grab the new handle.
🤖
Auto-Select Nearest Rod
Toggle Auto Rod in the HUD or press [Tab]. It selects the rod closest to the ball with 0.22s hysteresis (no flickering).
👥
2-Player Local Mode
Player 1 (Red): W/S (slide), A/D (switch rod), Space (kick).
Player 2 (Yellow): Arrow Up/Down (slide), Left/Right (switch rod), Enter (kick).
