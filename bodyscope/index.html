Open-Source Anatomy Credits & Licenses

BodyScope 3D incorporates authentic, open-licensed biomedical research geometry from leading worldwide open anatomical initiatives:

1. BodyParts3D (DBCLS, Japan)

Complete 206+ bone human skeleton reference dataset, featuring comprehensive neurocranium, facial viscerocranium, spine, ribs, pelvis, and individual finger/toe phalanges. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.1 Japan (CC BY-SA 2.1 JP).

2. Z-Anatomy (Open Anatomy Project)

Anatomically accurate 3D cardiovascular trees, respiratory bronchial systems, digestive viscera, and peripheral nervous network. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0).

3. NIH Visible Human Reference Project (NLM)

Reference cross-sectional anatomical dimensions and pelvic inlet dimorphism data. Public domain / US Government Work.

4. Terminologia Anatomica (TA2)

Official Latin anatomical nomenclature standardized by the Federative International Programme on Anatomical Terminologies (FIPAT).

Ethical Attribution: All adapted geometry conforms strictly to respective Creative Commons share-alike and attribution requirements.
